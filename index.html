WHAT IS THIS?

THE IDEA

Ink Pens is a pixel-art stationery drawer: ballpoints, gels, markers, fountain pens, brush pens — and the occasional quill — every one drawn pixel by pixel and dropped on paper, blueprints, blackboards and desks.

Out of 600,000+ possible trait combinations, exactly 3333 pens exist. No two share the same combination. Once they're minted, the metadata gets frozen on-chain — the drawer never changes.

No roadmap theater. Pens are $1.30 each on a brand-new chain, art you can actually look at, and a collection that's finished the day it sells out. Grab a pen, sign something.

Ink is Kraken's Ethereum L2 — fast, cheap, and young. Ink Pens is the kind of collection young chains are for: cheap enough that everyone's in, small enough that it matters.

FAQ

How do I mint?

On OpenSea. Connect any EVM wallet, make sure you're on the Ink network (your wallet will prompt you), and mint up to 3 per wallet at $1.30 per pen.

What's Ink and how do I get ETH there?

Ink is an Ethereum Layer 2 built by Kraken. Gas is paid in ETH and costs fractions of a cent. Easiest path: withdraw ETH from Kraken directly to Ink, or bridge from Ethereum mainnet.

Is the art really done?

Yes — all 3333 pens are rendered before mint. No "art coming soon". After sell-out the metadata is frozen on-chain, permanently.

What are the royalties?

5%, set on-chain via ERC-2981.

What do I get besides a pen?

A pen. A really nice pixel pen on a chain called Ink. That's the whole joke, and it only works 3333 times.
